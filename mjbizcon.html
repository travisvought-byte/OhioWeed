Plan your MJBizCon week.

The Attendee Planner & Connection Directory is a free Google Sheet for choosing who to meet at MJBizCon 2026, tracking follow-ups and building your schedule. It is a planning tool. Event details change, so check the event site before you travel.

Make your own copy first. The planner opens view-only. Choose File, then Make a copy in Google Sheets so your favorites and notes stay private in your own file.

Six steps from list to follow-up

  1. Find contacts. Browse the Directory tab, which lists companies and named speakers and can be filtered by business type and searched by specialty. On a phone, the Quick List shows every company, booth and offering in a narrow view.
  2. Favorite the ones that matter. Mark each contact you want to meet as a favorite. Favorites feed your plan.
  3. Reach out before the show. Use the draft intro note as a starting point, and the LinkedIn lookup where a contact lists one. Edit the note so it sounds like you.
  4. Track progress. For each contact, update Status, Next action and Reminder date so no follow-up slips.
  5. Plan your days. Use My Plan to set meeting dates and times. On Sessions & Events, mark forums, state takeovers and after-hours events as Going to build your schedule.
  6. Print and follow up. Print My Plan to carry on the floor, then send your follow-ups while the conversations are fresh.

Lists & GuideHow sources are tiered, how categories work and how the list is kept up to date.

Tips for a useful week

  • Decide your goal on Start Here before you favorite anyone. It tells you which contacts matter.
  • Keep your favorites short enough to actually visit. A long list is hard to work in a few days.
  • Send follow-ups within a day or two of each conversation, while you still remember the details.
  • Check the event site before you travel for schedule changes.
