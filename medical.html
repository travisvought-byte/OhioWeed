Get an Ohio medical card.

How patients register, where to find a certifying physician, help with the cost, and whether Medicaid or insurance pays. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card.

Not legal or medical advice. These are summaries of the Ohio Division of Cannabis Control’s own instructions, checked on the date shown. The Division’s page controls.

The steps

  1. Visit a physician certified to recommend medical cannabis, in person or by telemedicine. You need a visit at least once a year.
  2. The physician confirms a qualifying condition and enters the recommendation and your profile into the Patient & Caregiver Registry.
  3. Open the registry’s email, subject “ACTION NEEDED: Ohio MMCP Registry - Account Registration”, follow the link and activate your card.
  4. Buy at a licensed dispensary with your active card, active recommendation and government-issued ID.
  5. Renew each year after a physician visit.

Find a certifying physician

The State Medical Board of Ohio keeps the search for physicians who hold a certificate to recommend. Bring your Ohio driver’s license, Ohio ID card or another ID the Division approves, and review the qualifying conditions before the visit.

Help with the cost: veterans and low income

Ohio records indigent status and veteran status at registration. Ask your physician to mark it when they create your registry profile. If they did not, email proof to the Division at MMCPRegistry@com.ohio.gov before you activate the card.

Indigent status takes proof such as SSDI or SSI enrollment showing the monthly benefit. Veteran status takes an unexpired ID showing veteran status, such as a VA Veterans Identification Card.

Does Medicaid or insurance pay?

Patients pay for dispensary cannabis themselves. The VA does not pay for it, and says veterans are not denied VA benefits for using it. A few cannabis medicines approved by the FDA are ordinary prescriptions.

The national guide lists what Medicaid, Medicare, insurers, the VA and the IRS each say, with sources.

Caregivers

A registered caregiver may possess and administer medical cannabis for the patients listed on their registration. The recommending physician registers the caregiver, who then activates the registration by email the same way.

Where to buy and who to call

Use the dispensary lookup to find a licensed Ohio dispensary. For registry questions, the Division’s toll-free helpline is 1-833-464-6627.
