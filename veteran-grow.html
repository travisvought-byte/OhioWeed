Grow it. Finish it right.

Growing the plant is half the job. Trimming, drying, curing and making hash decide what you end up with. Travis Vought, a Marine veteran and Ohio home grower, is gauging interest in classes and harvest-day help for fellow veterans who grow at home.

This is an interest list, not a store. Nothing is sold here. Tell Travis what would help and he will follow up by email.

Ground rules

  • For adults 21 and older.
  • Ohio home grow is limited to six plants per adult and twelve per residence, grown at your own primary residence.
  • Your plants are processed at your home and stay there. Nobody grows or holds plants for someone else.
  • No sales of homegrown cannabis, ever.
  • No butane or other hydrocarbon extraction. Ohio prohibits it for home growers.
