Ohio veterans · 21 and older · home grow

Veterans who grow,
teaching each other.

Most people growing at home figured it out alone, off forums and guesswork. Travis Vought, a Marine veteran and Ohio home grower, is putting together classes and a group for veterans who want to learn the craft from someone who has done it, and to know a few other people doing the same thing.

Nothing here is for sale. This is about teaching and introductions between veterans who grow at their own homes. Tell Travis what you are after and he will follow up by email.
Want a heads up? Classes and meetups are announced by area. Get event email for your part of Ohio and you will hear when something is close enough to drive to.

Learn the part nobody teaches

Growing the plant is the half everyone writes about. Harvest timing, trimming, drying, curing and pressing hash are what decide whether a good plant turns into something worth having, and they are the part people get wrong.

Know a few other growers

A group of Ohio veterans who each grow at their own home, trading what works, lending equipment and answering each other's questions. No dues, no roster published anywhere, just introductions between people who are already doing this.

Harvest week is also when an extra pair of hands and the right equipment matter most. Travis is gauging interest in bringing trim gear and coaching to a fellow veteran's place for the day, so the work gets done properly and you learn how to do it yourself next year. Planned for 2027 if enough people want it.

How this works

  • Adults 21 and older.
  • Ohio allows six plants per adult and twelve per residence, at your own primary residence.
  • Your plants are your own, worked on at your own home. Nobody grows or holds plants for anyone else.
  • Solventless methods only. Ohio does not permit hydrocarbon extraction for home growers.
  • Nothing is bought or sold here, including instruction.

Classes and the group: veteranhomeguardians@gmail.com. Business, sponsorship or contracting: travis@vethomeguard.org.
