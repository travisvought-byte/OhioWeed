  2. Dec112026

    Law takes effectIn 16 days

    Federal hemp THC limit takes effect

    December 11, 2026 · U.S. Congress

    Hemp products with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per container lose federal legal status unless Congress acts first. In Ohio, intoxicating hemp is already limited to licensed dispensaries under SB 56, so expect fewer hemp THC drinks and gummies on sale.

June 2027

  1. Jun12027

    DeadlineIn about 6 months

    New packaging and label rules apply to every product

    June 1, 2027 · Ohio Division of Cannabis Control

    By this date, every product shipped to an Ohio dispensary must carry the universal THC symbol and the Division of Cannabis Control seal, with warnings printed on the package itself and child-resistant, tamper-evident packaging. Producers are switching over before then, so new labels will start showing up on shelves.

Recent Ohio cannabis news

  1. Nov242026

    News1 day ago

    Nearly 200 Ohio communities still block dispensaries

    November 24, 2026 · WVXU and WOSU

    Your city or township decides whether a dispensary can open there. Nearly 200 Ohio communities have moratoriums, while Hamilton and about 20 other southwest Ohio communities have said yes. Check your local rules before counting on a store nearby.

    WOSU ↗ Checked November 25, 2026

  2. Nov122026

    News13 days ago

    First phase of the federal hemp crackdown began

    November 12, 2026 · U.S. Congress

    Hemp products made with synthetic cannabinoids the plant can't produce on its own are no longer federally legal. The wider limit on hemp THC per container follows on December 11.

    Marijuana Moment ↗ Checked November 25, 2026

  3. Oct302026

    News26 days ago

    Ohio medical cannabis businesses apply for federal registration

    October 30, 2026 · U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration

    With state-licensed medical marijuana moved to Schedule III, DEA's list shows 125 Ohio dispensaries, 8 distributors and 27 manufacturers applied for federal registration. Businesses that applied in time keep operating under their state license while DEA reviews them.

    Marijuana Moment ↗ Checked November 25, 2026

  4. Oct72026

    News49 days ago

    New package and potency limits on adult-use products

    October 7, 2026 · Ohio Division of Cannabis Control

    Adult-use products are now capped at 90% THC. Flower is limited to 1 ounce per package, edibles to 10 mg per serving and 100 mg per package, and vapes to three 2-gram units per package. Expect smaller packages and possibly higher prices per milligram.

    ABC 6 On Your Side ↗ Checked November 25, 2026

  5. Oct52026

    News51 days ago

    Updated cannabis advertising rule took effect

    October 5, 2026 · Ohio Division of Cannabis Control

    Licensed businesses now follow the revised advertising rule. The Division of Cannabis Control has up to 21 business days to approve, deny or return each ad, so build that time into marketing plans.

    Ohio Division of Cannabis Control ↗ Checked November 25, 2026

  6. Sep262026

    News60 days ago

    Appeals court lets Ohio enforce part of its hemp limits

    September 26, 2026 · U.S. Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit

    The Sixth Circuit partly paused an order that had shielded ten out-of-state hemp drink makers, so Ohio can enforce some of its hemp limits against them while the appeal continues. Hemp drinks sold outside dispensaries remain legally uncertain.

    Forbes ↗ Checked November 25, 2026
