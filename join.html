Stay in the loop

Know when something is
happening near you.

Ohio is a big state and most of what happens here never makes it past word of mouth. Tell us which corner you are in and we will send a note when there is an event, a class or a veteran growers meetup close enough to drive to.

Sign up

The signup form goes live shortly. In the meantime, send an email with the part of Ohio you are in and you will be on the list when it opens.

Email to be added

One email at a time, only when something is actually happening. You can leave from the bottom of any message. The list is not sold, shared or handed to anyone.

What you will get

  • Events near you. Expos, markets, farmers markets with cannabis vendors, industry meetups and community days, pulled from the same feed as the events page.
  • Classes. Hash making, trimming, drying and curing. Mostly hands-on, mostly free, run by growers for growers. See the veteran growers page.
  • Veteran meetups. Small, informal, usually a handful of people who grow and want to compare notes.
  • Law changes worth acting on. When something moves at the Statehouse that you would want to write about, with a link to the letter builder.

Not a newsletter. Nothing to read every week. If a month goes by with nothing happening in your area, you will not hear from us that month.

All of Ohio

Pick this if you travel for the good ones, or if you are in a border county and could go either way.
